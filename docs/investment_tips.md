# Property Investment Tips

## Rental yield
Gross rental yield equals annual rent divided by purchase price. A yield of 4% to 8% is common in many markets. Net yield subtracts maintenance, vacancy, insurance, and taxes, and is the number that matters.

## Location drivers
Properties near transport links, schools, hospitals, and employment hubs hold value better and rent faster. Areas with new infrastructure projects often see appreciation, but check that projects are funded and underway rather than only announced.

## Risk management
Avoid putting all savings into a single property. Budget for vacancy periods of one to two months per year and for unexpected repairs. Prefer properties you could comfortably afford even if rent income stopped for a while. Be cautious about off-plan purchases: verify the developer's track record, approvals, and delivery history.

## Holding period
Real estate is illiquid. Selling can take months and costs several percent of the price, so investments usually need a holding period of five years or more to make sense.
