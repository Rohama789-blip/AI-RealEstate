# Legal Checklist for Property Transactions

## Verify ownership
Confirm the seller is the registered owner by checking the title deed or ownership record at the land registry. Ask for the full chain of previous transfers. Make sure the property has no mortgage, lien, or court dispute attached to it.

## Required documents
Typical documents include the title deed, a recent tax payment receipt, utility clearance certificates, an approved building plan, and identification of both parties. For apartments, also request the management society's no-objection certificate and maintenance fee statements.

## Contracts and transfer
Use a written sale agreement that names both parties, the exact property description, price, payment schedule, handover date, and penalties for breach. Pay through traceable bank transfers rather than cash. Have a lawyer review the agreement before signing and complete the transfer at the official registry so that the new ownership is recorded.

## Taxes and fees
Expect stamp duty or transfer tax, registration fees, and possibly capital gains tax if you sell within a short period. Rules differ by region, so confirm current rates with a lawyer or the tax authority.
