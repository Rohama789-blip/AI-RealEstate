# Area Guide (sample data)

## Downtown
Dense, walkable, close to offices and nightlife. Mostly apartments with premium prices and strong rental demand, but limited parking and gardens.

## Riverside
Quiet residential area with parks and a riverfront path. Popular with families, good schools, and steady price growth.

## Greenfield
Newer suburban development with houses and townhouses, mid-range prices, and larger plots. Longer commute to the center.

## Lakeview
Attractive views and a calm environment. Prices are above average, especially for villas and larger homes.

## Old Town
Older buildings, lower prices per square foot, and character. Expect higher maintenance costs and check building age and condition carefully.

## Hillcrest
Elevated area with good air quality and views. Mixed houses and villas, moderately high prices.

## Tech Park
Close to the business district and tech employers. Strong rental demand from professionals, mostly modern apartments.

## Seaside
Highest prices per square foot. Premium villas and apartments with sea views; check for humidity-related maintenance and flood risk.
